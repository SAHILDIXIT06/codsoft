Simple Linear Regression: TV Advertising vs Sales
==================================================

Objective:
----------
Use the advertising dataset from ISLR (Introduction to Statistical Learning) to analyze 
the relationship between TV advertising expenditure and sales using a simple linear 
regression model.

Dataset Description:
-------------------
The dataset contains advertising budget allocations (in thousands of dollars) and 
corresponding sales figures:

- TV: Advertising budget spent on TV (in thousands of dollars)
- Radio: Advertising budget spent on Radio (in thousands of dollars)  
- Newspaper: Advertising budget spent on Newspaper (in thousands of dollars)
- Sales: Product sales in thousands of units (TARGET VARIABLE)

Focus:
------
This analysis focuses specifically on the relationship between:
- Independent Variable (X): TV Advertising Budget
- Dependent Variable (Y): Sales

Problem Statement:
------------------
Analyze and model the linear relationship between TV advertising expenditure and 
product sales to:
1. Understand the strength and nature of the relationship
2. Build a predictive model using simple linear regression
3. Predict sales based on TV advertising budget
4. Provide actionable insights for marketing budget allocation

Tasks:
------
1. Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on TV and Sales
2. Calculate correlation between TV advertising and sales
3. Visualize the relationship using scatter plots
4. Build a simple linear regression model (Y = β₀ + β₁X)
5. Evaluate model performance (R², RMSE, MAE)
6. Interpret the regression coefficients
7. Make predictions on new TV advertising budgets
8. Check model assumptions (linearity, normality, homoscedasticity)
9. Provide business insights and recommendations

Expected Deliverables:
---------------------
1. Statistical analysis of TV-Sales relationship
2. Linear regression equation: Sales = β₀ + β₁ × TV
3. Model performance metrics
4. Visualizations (scatter plot, regression line, residual plots)
5. Predictions for different TV advertising budgets
6. Business recommendations based on findings
